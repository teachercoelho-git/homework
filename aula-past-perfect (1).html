Grammar Focus — The Past Perfect

Form: subject + had + past participle (e.g., had finished, had gone, had never seen).

When we use it

  • 1. An action completed before another past action or moment — "By the time Karl arrived, Manoel had already left."
  • 2. Reported speech / narrative background — explaining what happened earlier in a story.
  • 3. Regret or unreal past conditions — "I wish I had known sooner."
  • 4. With time markers like already, never, by the time, before, after, until.

Common mistake to avoid

Don't confuse it with the simple past. "He had eaten" shows the eating finished before another past reference point. "He ate" is just one past action with no second reference point.
